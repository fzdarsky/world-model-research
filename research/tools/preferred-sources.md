## Academic Papers & Preprints

### Preprint Servers

- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org — Primary server for ML/AI/CV/robotics research
- **TechRxiv**: https://www.techrxiv.org — IEEE's preprint server for engineering, applied CS, and technology; covers applied research and industry use cases that may not appear on arXiv

### Search Queries

**Architectures & Methods** (search on arXiv + TechRxiv):

- **JEPA**: `"joint embedding predictive architecture"` OR `"JEPA"`
- **Energy-Based Models**: `"energy based models"` OR `"energy-based models"`
- **Dragon Hatchling**: `"baby dragon hatchling"` OR `"BDH"` OR `"dragon hatchling"`
- **World Models (general)**: `"world models"` (broad — covers RL, robotics, simulation, etc.)
- **Self-Supervised**: `"self-supervised learning"` + `"representation"`

**Applications & Use Cases** (search on arXiv + TechRxiv + Google Scholar):

- **Healthcare / Medical**: `"world models"` + (`"medical"` OR `"clinical"` OR `"healthcare"`)

## Monitoring Strategy

When `/sources` is run, check:

1. **Preprint searches** (last 30 days) - Search both arXiv and TechRxiv for papers matching architecture AND application queries
2. **GitHub organizations** - Check for new repos or major releases
3. **Company blogs — research labs** - Scan for world models/JEPA/EBM/BDH mentions
4. **Company blogs — applied & industry** - Scan for world model applications, digital twins, Physical AI deployments
5. **Researcher pages** - Check for new publications from key researchers
6. **YouTube videos** - Look for talks from / interviews with / news about key players on world models/JEPA/EBM/BDH

Present top 5-10 most relevant findings and ask which to add. Balance foundational research and applied/engineering content.
